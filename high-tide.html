Beaches for wave-catchers

Whether your a family of older kids who like to ride the waves or someone who wants to try out surfing, you're going to be looking for a beach with wilder waves than others.

  • Brooks Street

    Brooks Street, seen in the photo, is known in the community for being an iconic surfing spot. The waves curl high and hard, which is great for beach-goers on the more active side! These gorgeous waves are pulled by two coral reefs.

  • El Moro

    El Moro is a piece of the larger coast within Crystal Cove State Park. You'll find surfers, skimboarders, boogie-boarders, and others alike riding the top of the clear blue waves. This beach offers much more than sand and swimming, though, with a near campsite and many scenic walking paths. If the wreckage of the waves gets too tiring, there's also. smaller tide pools to enjoy near the shoreline!

  • Rockpile Beach

    The Rockpile Beach is decoracted with rocky cliffs and rocks in the sand and in the water. This Beach is mostly popular with surfers, but the waves can sometimes calm enough for a swim in the shallow end!

Always remember that tides and waves are unpredictable. It's always smart to check the activity of the water at your beach of choice online before getting into the water, and to keep an eye on any loved ones in the water at all times. Beaches are fun, but they're better when your safe and prepared!
