The city of Laguna beach is a charming city in Southern California, found a couple miles south-east of the popular Newport Beach. The city is home to many, many miles of state-protected beaches for locals and tourists alike to enjoy. There's a good reason so many people visit, but if you're unsure which of the many beaches is fit for you and your family on vacation, this is the perfect guide for you!

For more information on the city itself, Laguna Beach has it's very own website!
