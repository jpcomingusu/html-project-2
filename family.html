Many families, like my own, prefer to vacation on a sandy beach over a rowdy amusement park. So, of course, many families wind up scouring the internet for beaches with calm waters that are kid friendly. Well, scoure no more, because I have three great options for families with children of all ages!

  • Shaws Cove

    Shaws Cove, pictured above, is note-worthy based on it's calm, lax waters. You'll rarely find waves breaking on this shoreline. This beach is known as a beginner friendly "diving beach", and is perfect for swimmers who like to break out the snorkle gear!

  • Aliso Beach

    Aliso Beach offers families more space on the sand than other shorelines in Laguna, making this one a great option for the kiddos that enjoy time in the sand just as much as the water! There's a band of lifegaurds on duty here, not to mention a playground built into the sand for smaller kids!
