Beaches Suitable For the Whole Family

Many families, like my own, prefer to vacation on a sandy beach over a rowdy amusement park. So, of course, many families wind up scouring the internet for beaches with calm waters that are kid friendly. Well, scoure no more, because I have three great options for families with children of all ages!

  • Shaws Cove

    Shaws Cove, as pictured above, is note-worthy based on it's calm, lax waters. You'll rarely find waves breaking on this shoreline. This beach is known as a beginner friendly "diving beach", and is perfect for swimmers who like to break out the snorkle gear!

  • Aliso Beach

    Aliso Beach offers families more space on the sand than other shorelines in Laguna, making this one a great option for the kiddos that enjoy time in the sand just as much as the water! There's a band of lifegaurds on duty here, not to mention a playground built into the sand for smaller kids!

  • "Main" Beach

    The "Main" Beach, AKA Laguna Beach, is popular for a reason! This beach has a charming wooden boardwalk along the cliff for those who simply like a good view. Laguna Beach also has a playground, like Aliso Beach, but this one isn't built into the sand. You'll also find some outdoor basketball courts that may interest the families' teenagers, who may be "too old" to play in the sand.

Although these beaches are fun and on the calmer side, always remember that tides and waves are unpredictable. It's always smart to check the activity of the water at your beach of choice online before getting into the water, and to keep an eye on any loved ones in the water at all times. Beaches are fun, but they're better when your safe and prepared!
